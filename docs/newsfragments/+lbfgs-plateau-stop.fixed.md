An L-BFGS session driven by the caller's own `Solver::step` loop (C `rgmin_solver_step`, `rgmin_solver_step_fg`, Python `Solver.step`) with no iteration budget (`maxiter = 0`) stops on a plateau the objective cannot resolve. After `max(n, 2)` consecutive accepted steps that move `x`, keep the value inside the objective roundoff window (`set_objective_roundoff`, else four epsilon relative) and keep the gradient norm within `sqrt(eps)` relative of the first of them while it is still at or above `gtol`, the step reports `Status::StepTolerance` (C `RGMIN_STOP_STEP_TOLERANCE`). Before, such a fit reported `Iterating` forever: on the default capped eOn step and on the strong Wolfe opt-in, whose approximate Wolfe test accepts steps of about `1e-10` with no measurable decrease. A single flat step, such as an overshoot, does not stop the fit. A positive `maxiter` reports what it did before, and `Solver::run`, `minimize_lbfgs` and the C and Python one-shots still read `maxiter = 0` as no steps. The C signature and ABI minor 43 are unchanged.
